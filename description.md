# Chaos Machine

## Short description (max 150 characters)

Sneak a cute mech into a neon fortress. Snipe guards, strike from the shadows, then go loud with minigun and rockets when the alarm sounds.

(139 characters)

## Description (max 500 characters)

You are the Chaos Machine: a small mech raiding sky fortresses for salvage. Snipe from your perch, grapple over walls, shoot ropes, take guards from behind, burn the evidence and hide as a toaster. Stop the runner before the beacon is lit. Strip the salvage, outwit the Warden, and fly out on your robo dog. If the alarm sounds, the minigun and rockets come out. Three fortresses, upgrades, and a base that learns your habits.

(424 characters)

## How to play

1. A short training ground comes first: ten small rooms, each shown by a
   ghost of your mech before you try it (the button replays it). Then you land
   on a perch above the fortress. Look around: guards patrol (some stop to
   chat), sentries watch from the towers, drones and searchlights sweep the
   yard, an Engineer mends what you break, and from level III a Scrap Hound
   follows your scent. Hold the scope on anyone for two seconds to tag them:
   their round is drawn and they show through walls.
2. Stay unseen. The cones on the ground show where each guard is looking. A
   guard who glimpses you fills a white mark, then a yellow one as it walks
   over to look; red, it is sure, shouts, and somebody runs for an alarm
   beacon. Stop the runner, or sabotage the beacons first (E), and the alarm
   never sounds. Cover hides you when you crouch; shadow makes you harder to
   see; crouched in a thicket you are not seen at all unless a guard is right
   on top of you; and the hide box (B) works while you keep still. Bodies can
   be dragged and tucked into a container (E). Lost, the guards search where
   they last saw you, then go back to their rounds, warier.
3. Thin them out quietly: a scoped headshot is silent (a round to the body
   takes two, to the legs three, and the guard who lives sends others looking),
   and a guard you reach from behind or the side (or from a ledge above) can
   be taken with the blade.
4. Throw a holographic decoy to pull a patrol away from where you want to go.
   Walls are no obstacle: the grappling hook (G) takes you up, but its bite is
   heard nearby.
5. Find the glowing piles of salvage (the marker points to the nearest) and
   strip them (E). Three are needed at level I, one more at each level after.
6. The bosses do not fall to one round, no blade takes them, and the first
   hit on one brings the whole base. Prepare in stealth: cut the generator
   that powers the Warden's shield (level IV), jam the Foreman's servo on its
   pad while it sleeps (level II), lure the Goliath (level III) into shelling
   a decoy at its own feet. Past half health the Warden drops his shield and
   dashes at you after a glint: step aside. Three shells and the Goliath
   overheats, its open vents swinging toward you. Hit a drone in its eye: a
   round in its shell only makes it wary.
7. If the alarm sounds, the stealth is over: reinforcements arrive and the
   Enforcers power up. Switch to the minigun (aim for their glowing joints)
   and the rocket launcher. Your armor mends when you break out of the fire.
8. With the salvage in hand, run to the green beacon: your robo dog folds into
   a jet board and flies you out.
9. In the Command Base, choose an upgrade in the Armory or the Chassis Bay and
   make it in the smithy: keep the furnace's heat in the band, strike the anvil
   on the beat, quench at cherry red. Better work, more stars, scrap back.
   Each cleared threat level opens the next, up to level V. The piles and the
   patrols change from raid to raid, and the base answers your habits: too
   many headshots and the guards wear helmets; the threat card says what it
   has learned.

Medals for every clear: **Ghost** (never detected), **Marksman** (70 % of your
rounds on target) and **Swift** (inside the time limit).

## Controls

### Phone and tablet

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Drag on the left side | Move (a stick appears under your thumb) |
| Drag on the right side | Look around |
| Hold the scope on a unit for two seconds | Tag it (its round is drawn, it shows through walls) |
| Hold still on the right side | Raise the weapon (the sniper's scope) |
| Let go | Fire (the minigun fires while you hold) |
| Tap on the right side, near a guard | Strike with the blade |
| Buttons, bottom right | Weapons, decoy, hide box, sprint, crouch |
| Hook / salvage button (appears when there is one) | Grappling hook / strip the pile |
| ✕ while aiming | Lower the weapon without firing |
| Fire / zoom chips while aiming | Fire / zoom the scope a notch |
| ? (top right) | Show the control hints again |

### Mouse and keyboard

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| W A S D (or the arrow keys ↑ ↓) | Move |
| Mouse | Look around (click the game once to capture the mouse) |
| Right mouse button (hold) | Aim / scope |
| Left mouse button | Fire |
| Shift (hold) | Sprint |
| E | Strike with the blade / strip a pile / sabotage a beacon or the boss's power / drag a body |
| G | Grappling hook: up the wall in front, or onto the wall top you look at (again to recall it) |
| Space | Jump (into a ledge: catch it and climb) |
| T | Rope gun: from up high, a rope to the spot you look at |
| X | Flamethrower: burn a body or a wreck to ash, so nobody finds it |
| C | Crouch / stand |
| B | Disguise on / off (the toaster; more in the Chassis Bay) |
| Q | Throw a decoy |
| 1 / 2 / 3, mouse wheel or Tab | Sniper rifle / minigun / rocket launcher |
| Mouse wheel while scoped | Zoom the scope in and out |
| R | Reload |
| Esc or P | Pause |
| H or F1 | Show the lessons again |
| F2 | Mute |

Keys are shown in your own keyboard layout and can be changed in Options →
Controls. Ladders and cables are used by walking into them.
