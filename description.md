# Chaos Machine

## Short description (max 150 characters)

Sneak a cute mech into a neon fortress. Snipe guards, strike from the shadows, then go loud with minigun and rockets when the alarm sounds.

(139 characters)

## Description (max 500 characters)

You are the Chaos Machine: a small mech raiding a floating scrapyard fortress for salvage. Snipe sentries from your perch, grapple over walls, take guards from behind with your blade and hide in a box when patrols come looking. Strip the salvage, dodge the Warden, and let your robo dog fly you out. If the alarm goes off, the minigun and rockets come out. Forge your haul into upgrades in the smithy and return at a higher threat level.

(437 characters)

## How to play

1. You land on a perch above the fortress. Look around: guards patrol, sentries
   watch from the towers, drones sweep searchlights over the yard.
2. Stay unseen. The cones on the ground show where each guard is looking;
   cover hides you when you crouch, and the hide box (B) hides you anywhere
   while you keep still. The meter at the top is the base's Alert Level: a
   sighting, a found body or a missed shot raises it. A guard's radio that goes
   quiet gets noticed: half a minute after a kill somebody comes to look.
3. Thin them out quietly: a scoped headshot is silent, and a guard you reach
   from behind (or from a ledge above) can be taken with the blade.
4. Throw a holographic decoy to pull a patrol away from where you want to go.
   Walls are no obstacle: the grappling hook (G) takes you up, but its bite is
   heard nearby.
5. Find the glowing piles of salvage (the marker points to the nearest) and
   strip them (E). Three are needed at level I, one more at each level after.
6. The bosses do not fall to one round, and the first hit on one brings the
   whole base: the Warden on the keep's roof (an armoured helm, and a riot
   shield from level IV), the Foreman at level II, the Goliath Tank from level
   III. Thin out the guards and drones first. Hit a drone in its eye: a round
   in its shell only makes it wary.
7. If the alarm sounds, the stealth is over: reinforcements arrive and the
   Enforcers power up. Switch to the minigun (aim for their glowing joints)
   and the rocket launcher. Your armor mends when you break out of the fire.
8. With the salvage in hand, run to the green beacon: your robo dog folds into
   a jet board and flies you out.
9. In the Command Base, choose an upgrade in the Armory or the Chassis Bay and
   make it in the smithy: keep the furnace's heat in the band, strike the anvil
   on the beat, quench at cherry red. Better work, more stars, scrap back.
   Each cleared threat level opens the next, up to level V.

Medals for every clear: **Ghost** (never detected), **Marksman** (70 % of your
rounds on target) and **Swift** (inside the time limit).

## Controls

### Phone and tablet

| Do this | To |
|---|---|
| Drag on the left side | Move (a stick appears under your thumb) |
| Drag on the right side | Look around |
| Hold still on the right side | Raise the weapon (the sniper's scope) |
| Let go | Fire (the minigun fires while you hold) |
| Tap on the right side, near a guard | Strike with the blade |
| Buttons, bottom right | Weapons, decoy, hide box, sprint, crouch |
| Hook / salvage button (appears when there is one) | Grappling hook / strip the pile |
| ✕ while aiming | Lower the weapon without firing |
| ? (top right) | Show the control hints again |

### Mouse and keyboard

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| W A S D (or the arrow keys ↑ ↓) | Move |
| Mouse | Look around (click the game once to capture the mouse) |
| Right mouse button (hold) | Aim / scope |
| Left mouse button | Fire |
| Shift (hold) | Sprint |
| E | Strike with the blade / strip a pile of salvage |
| G | Grappling hook (again to recall it) |
| C | Crouch / stand |
| B | Hide box on / off |
| Q | Throw a decoy |
| 1 / 2 / 3, mouse wheel or Tab | Sniper rifle / minigun / rocket launcher |
| R | Reload |
| Esc or P | Pause |
| F2 | Mute |

Keys are shown in your own keyboard layout and can be changed in Options →
Controls. Ladders and cables are used by walking into them.
