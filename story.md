# Chaos Machine — the story

The game tells its story without words: an intro, a short scene each time a
fortress falls, a salvage beat after the first raids and an outro. Everything
is shown by what happens on screen, never written or spoken. This file is the
source those scenes come from (round 4, #100).

## The world

Islands of rock float over a sea of cloud. Long ago a war was fought up here
with machines the size of hills; what is left of them lies everywhere, and
scrap is the only thing worth anything. A cartel of salvage lords has walled
the best islands into fortresses, drafted crews of guards, and keeps the
scrap for itself: melting it into new war machines that patrol the sky.

## Who is who

- **The Chaos Machine.** A small salvage mech built from parts nobody else
  wanted, with a sniper rifle, a mini-gun, a rocket launcher, a blade, a
  grappling hook and a rope gun. It lives in the Mobile Command Base, a
  floating workshop that drifts from island to island.
- **The robo dog.** The mech's companion: it folds into a jet board, flies the
  mech in, and hauls the loot home.
- **The Warden.** The cartel's enforcer, in an armoured helm with a red visor
  and (later) a riot shield. He holds the Bastion.
- **The Foreman.** A marked Enforcer walker who runs the Foundry, where the
  scrap is melted down and cast into Goliath tanks.
- **The garrisons.** Guards, sentries, Engineers, drones, searchlights, Scrap
  Hounds, Roombas, grenadiers, shield guards and marksmen: crews, not
  monsters. They chat, they get bored, they are afraid of the dark.

## The arc

1. **The Bastion.** The mech takes back scrap from the cartel's mountain
   stronghold. When the Bastion falls (threat III), the Warden escapes on an
   airship toward smoke on the horizon: the Foundry.
2. **The Foundry.** Rooms cut out of a block of works, where Bastion scrap
   becomes Goliaths. When it falls, the furnaces go cold and the last cast
   hull is loaded onto a cargo ship marked with the Warden's insignia.
3. **The Harbour.** The cartel's dockyard on the edge of the islands, where
   the Warden's flagship takes the war machines out to the other islands.
   When it falls, the flagship slips its moorings and makes for the Spire.
4. **The Spire** (round 5). The cartel's signal tower: five terraces one in
   the other, its Watchtower lamp sweeping the rings, the Airship circling it.
   Its fall at threat III ends the campaign: the flagship breaks from its
   moorings and drifts down into the cloud, and the islands' scrap is free.

## The scenes

Every scene can be skipped from its first frame (a skip button, or Space held
for a second and a half, #175), plays once, and can be replayed from the Command Base.
The gameplay bracket pauses while a scene plays.

### Intro (about 50 s; once, after the training ground, before the first Bastion raid)

An ability showcase on a piece of the Bastion, wordless, the music's hero
theme under it:

1. Over the cloud sea at dusk, the robo dog's jet board skims the clouds with
   the mech crouched on it; it hops off onto a shelf and the board flies away.
2. A cliff: the mech throws the hook, is pulled up, rolls over the edge.
3. It runs to the wall over the yard and throws a decoy down: the yard guard
   turns to it, his back to the wall.
4. It shoots a rope across the yard, runs it, and drops from it onto the
   guard: the kill from above.
5. Up the ladder onto the wall; the rifle: a slow zoom, one headshot on the
   sentry on his tower.
6. The mini-gun spins up and shreds the drone. The base hears it: the wall
   guard, a Roomba, an Enforcer, the searchlight, the Scrap Hound and the
   Goliath all come for it, the Warden at the tank.
7. A rocket into the Goliath; it goes down, smoking.
8. The hook up the perch tower. The Airship rises out of the clouds and
   opens up with everything: both turrets, both rocket pods. The mech ducks
   behind the perch's parapet as it is blown apart round it.
9. The robo dog's board swoops in low; the mech leaps onto it and they are
   gone, every round behind them, the Airship turning after them.
10. A still of the third fortress, the Harbour, for three seconds: what is
    coming. Cut to the game.

### Salvage (10 s; after the first clear of Bastion threat I and of threat II, before the rewards)

#103. The fight is over. The mech strips the wrecked Enforcers, pulls plates
off a burst tank, sweeps a scrap pile into the robo dog's hold; the dog's
board hums, loaded, and lifts off toward the Command Base.

### A fortress falls (12 to 20 s; the first time each fortress is cleared at threat III)

- **The Bastion:** the keep's roof, smoke; the Warden runs for an airship
  moored at the curtain wall; it pulls away toward a column of furnace smoke
  on the horizon. The mech watches from the roof, the dog at its side. Tension
  cue.
- **The Foundry:** the furnaces die one by one; the last Goliath hull, half
  cast, is craned onto a cargo ship bearing the Warden's red visor mark; the
  ship drifts off toward the island's edge. Tension cue.

### Outro (about 40 s; after the Spire falls at threat III)

(Round 5: it used to wait on a Harbour threat V that no longer exists; it is
still staged on the Harbour's quay, see the round 5 deferred list.)

The flagship breaks loose and sinks slowly into the cloud, the Warden's red
visor the last thing visible. Scrap rains on the quay. The robo dog unfolds
its board; the mech steps on; they fly back to the Command Base, which lights
up as they land, the hold full. The base drifts on toward new islands at
sunrise. A warm finale cue.

### The first raid's opening (#104)

The very first Bastion raid begins with a scripted moment that teaches by
doing: the mech lands on the battery's ledge behind a guard (the ledge is
longer and a crate hides the landing), sneaks up on its own, takes the guard
down from behind, then shoulders the rifle and drops a guard standing across
the rope's far end. Then the player has control.

## Music cues

Three synthesised cues, mixed soft (leads low-passed near 7 kHz, 2 to 5 kHz
tamed, about −18 LUFS with a −1 dB ceiling):

- **Hero theme:** the intro, and the outro's flight home.
- **Tension:** a fortress falls.
- **Warm finale:** the outro's last scene.
